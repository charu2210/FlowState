## 5. Labelling Validity

The time-block pseudo-labelling strategy assumes monotonic fatigue accumulation (Ackerman & Kanfer, 2009). Validity is checked via:

1. **NASA-TLX correlation**: Pearson r between model anomaly score and Weighted Workload Score (collected every 10 min).
2. **Cohen's κ agreement**: Between pseudo-labels and TLX-derived labels. κ > 0.60 (substantial agreement) validates the approach.
3. **Induced-load manipulation check**: In dual-task sessions, secondary task accuracy should drop in high-load phases (confirms the manipulation was effective).
